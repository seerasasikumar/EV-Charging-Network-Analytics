# 📊 Business Insights

# EV Charging Network Analytics

This document summarizes the major business insights obtained from the EV Charging Network Analytics project using Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI.

---

# 📈 Executive Summary

The analysis of 500 EV charging sessions reveals valuable information about revenue generation, energy consumption, customer behavior, and charging demand across different cities.

These insights can help business stakeholders optimize charging station operations and plan future expansion.

---

# 💰 Revenue Analysis

## Insight 1

Delhi generated the highest revenue among all cities.

**Business Impact**

- Delhi has the highest business potential.
- Future investment should prioritize this location.

---

## Insight 2

Jaipur and Kolkata were the second and third highest revenue-generating cities.

**Business Impact**

- These cities have strong customer demand.
- Marketing campaigns can further increase revenue.

---

# ⚡ Energy Consumption Analysis

## Insight 3

Delhi consumed the highest charging energy.

**Business Impact**

- High-capacity charging infrastructure is required.
- Additional fast chargers may improve service quality.

---

## Insight 4

Energy consumption closely followed revenue trends.

**Business Impact**

- Higher energy usage directly contributes to increased revenue.

---

# 🚗 Vehicle Analysis

## Insight 5

Truck charging sessions were the highest.

**Business Impact**

- Commercial vehicles contribute significantly to charging demand.
- Dedicated truck charging bays should be considered.

---

## Insight 6

Cars also contributed a large number of charging sessions.

**Business Impact**

- Passenger vehicle infrastructure should continue expanding.

---

# 💳 Payment Analysis

## Insight 7

Cash was the most preferred payment method.

**Business Impact**

- Cash payment facilities should remain available.
- Digital payment adoption can be encouraged through offers and rewards.

---

# ⏰ Peak Hour Analysis

## Insight 8

The highest charging demand occurred at **6 AM**.

**Business Impact**

- Additional staff should be available during peak hours.
- Charging station maintenance should be scheduled during low-demand periods.

---

# 📊 Operational Recommendations

- Expand charging stations in Delhi.
- Increase charger capacity in high-demand cities.
- Improve infrastructure for commercial vehicles.
- Promote digital payment methods.
- Optimize workforce scheduling during peak hours.
- Monitor charging demand regularly using Power BI dashboards.

---

# 🚀 Future Opportunities

- Predict charging demand using Machine Learning.
- Forecast revenue and energy consumption.
- Detect seasonal charging trends.
- Analyze customer loyalty.
- Build real-time monitoring dashboards using live EV charging data.

---

# ✅ Conclusion

The EV Charging Network Analytics project demonstrates how Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI can be integrated to transform raw charging station data into meaningful business insights.

The findings support data-driven decision-making, operational optimization, and strategic planning for EV charging network growth.
