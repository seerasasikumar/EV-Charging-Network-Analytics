# EV Charging Network Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

This folder contains an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Electric Vehicle (EV) charging station performance. The dashboard provides insights into charging sessions, revenue, energy consumption, customer behavior, and operational performance.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to help stakeholders monitor charging station performance, identify revenue-generating cities, understand customer payment preferences, analyze vehicle charging trends, and support business decision-making.

---

# 🛠️ Tools Used

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX
- Interactive Visualizations

---

# 📊 Dashboard KPIs

- Total Charging Sessions
- Total Revenue
- Total Energy Consumed
- Average Charging Cost
- Average Energy per Session

---

# 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### Revenue by City

Shows the total revenue generated by each city.

### Charging Sessions by Vehicle Type

Displays the number of charging sessions for each vehicle category.

### Revenue by Payment Method

Analyzes customer payment preferences using a donut chart.

### Energy Consumption by City

Shows total energy consumed across different cities.

### Peak Charging Hours

Displays charging demand by hour to identify peak operating times.

### Business Summary

Highlights:

- Top Revenue City
- Top Energy City
- Top Vehicle Type
- Most Preferred Payment Method
- Peak Charging Hour

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Delhi generated the highest revenue.
- Delhi recorded the highest energy consumption.
- Trucks had the highest charging sessions.
- Cash was the most preferred payment method.
- Peak charging activity occurred at 6 AM.
