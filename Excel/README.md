# 📊 Excel Analysis

## 📌 Overview

This folder contains the Microsoft Excel analysis of the **EV Charging Network Analytics** project. The analysis focuses on charging sessions, revenue generation, energy consumption, vehicle types, payment methods, and charging patterns through an interactive dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand the operational performance of EV charging stations and identify opportunities to improve revenue, optimize energy utilization, and enhance customer experience.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- KPI Cards
- Dashboard Design

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Charging Sessions
- Total Revenue (₹)
- Total Energy Consumed (kWh)
- Average Charging Cost (₹)
- Average Energy per Session (kWh)

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

- 📍 Revenue by City
- 🚗 Charging Sessions by Vehicle Type
- 💳 Revenue by Payment Method
- ⚡ Energy Consumption by City
- 🕒 Peak Charging Hours

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides interactive slicers for:

- City
- Vehicle Type
- Payment Method

These filters enable users to analyze charging network performance dynamically.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Delhi generated the highest charging revenue.
- Trucks recorded the highest number of charging sessions.
- Cash was the most preferred payment method.
- Delhi also recorded the highest energy consumption.
- Peak charging demand occurred during daytime hours, indicating high station utilization during business hours.
